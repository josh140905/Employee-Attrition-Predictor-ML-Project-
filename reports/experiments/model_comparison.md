## Executive Summary

As part of RetainIQ's machine learning continuous improvement program, we conducted controlled model experiments to improve employee attrition detection. Experiments were executed exclusively on the 80% training partition ($N=1,176$, 190 attrition cases, 16.16% base rate) using leak-free **Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation** with identical fold splits across all candidate architectures.

### Key Experimental Findings
1. **Random Forest Tuning (+2.57% PR-AUC):** A 324-candidate grid search (1,620 fits) on unweighted Random Forest improved CV PR-AUC from **0.5563** (Default) to **0.5820** (Tuned) using `n_estimators=300, max_depth=14, max_features='log2', min_samples_split=5, min_samples_leaf=1`.
2. **Directional Monotonic Constraints (Eliminated 100% of What-If Inconsistencies):** In unconstrained gradient boosted trees, **10.12% of employees (119 cases)** exhibited counter-intuitive predictions where assigning overtime *reduced* predicted attrition risk. Applying native monotonic constraints (`monotonic_cst=+1` on OverTime, `-1` on satisfaction indices) guaranteed **0.00% violations (100% consistency)** while maintaining competitive generalization (PR-AUC 0.5679).
3. **Probability-Averaging Ensemble (Top Performer: 0.6547 PR-AUC, 0.6263 Recall, 0.5965 F1):** Blending Logistic Regression and Tuned Random Forest probabilities ($P_{\text{ens}} = 0.55 \cdot P_{\text{LR}} + 0.45 \cdot P_{\text{RF}}$) outperformed all individual models, achieving a CV PR-AUC of **0.6576 (+/- 0.0447)** (OOF PR-AUC **0.6547**), representing a **+2.22% gain over Logistic Regression** (0.6325) and **+7.27% gain over Tuned Random Forest** (0.5820).

## 1. Experimental Protocol & Strict Governance Rules

All experiments were governed by strict machine learning and AI governance constraints:
- **No Protected Attribute Leakage:** `Gender`, `MaritalStatus`, and `Age` were strictly excluded from the predictive feature matrix $X$ (32 total features).
- **Strictly No Class Weights:** All models were trained with `class_weight=None` (no artificial loss multipliers or sample weighting).
- **Strict Holdout Isolation:** The 294-row held-out test partition ($N=294$, 47 positive attrition labels) remained completely untouched. Zero tuning, threshold selection, or evaluation was performed on holdout data.
- **Identical Cross-Validation Splits:** All models were evaluated across the exact same 5 folds generated by `StratifiedKFold(n_splits=5, shuffle=True, random_state=42)` on the training set.
- **Production Safety:** The active Day 3 production model (`models/calibrated_model_day2_age_excluded.pkl`) and application (`app/main.py`) remain completely untouched.

### 3.2 Experiment 2: Gradient Boosting & Directional Monotonic Constraints
Standard gradient boosted trees construct splits greedily based on impurity or gradient reduction. In small or imbalanced HR datasets, this leads to spurious interaction splits that violate human domain logic.

We evaluated what-if sensitivity by holding all employee features fixed and perturbing individual operational attributes:

| **HistGB (Unconstrained)** | 119 / 1,176 | 10.12% | 89.88% | 85 / 1,176 | 7.23% | 92.77% |
| **HistGB (OverTime Monotonic)** | **0 / 1,176** | **0.00%** | **100.00%** | 82 / 1,176 | 6.97% | 93.03% |

#### Why Monotonic Constraints Matter for HR Governance:
- In the unconstrained model, an HR manager performing scenario planning would find that for **119 employees**, requiring them to work overtime *decreased* their flight risk.
- Imposing `monotonic_cst=+1` on `OverTime` and `Overtime_LowWLB`, and `-1` on satisfaction indices completely eliminated this behavior (**100% directional consistency**).
- Generalization was preserved: Domain Full Monotonic achieved **0.5679 PR-AUC** (matching unconstrained at 0.5675) with superior precision at optimal threshold (**0.5783 vs 0.4377**).

### 3.3 Experiment 3: Probability-Averaging Ensemble
We combined the continuous linear log-odds modeling of Logistic Regression with the non-linear interaction modeling of Tuned Random Forest:
$$P_{\text{ens}}(w) = w \cdot P_{\text{LR}}(X) + (1 - w) \cdot P_{\text{RF}}(X)$$

#### Grid Search Over LR Weight $w \in [0.0, 1.0]$ on Training Out-Of-Fold Predictions:
- At $w=0.0$ (Tuned RF alone): OOF PR-AUC = 0.5632
- At $w=0.50$ (Simple Equal Average): OOF PR-AUC = 0.6539
- At $w^* = 0.55$ (Optimal Weight): OOF PR-AUC = **0.6547**
- At $w=1.0$ (Baseline LR alone): OOF PR-AUC = 0.6312

#### Why the Ensemble Wins:
- Logistic Regression provides well-calibrated global linear slopes across continuous metrics (income, tenure, promotion lag).
- Random Forest captures non-linear local interaction patterns (overtime burnout within specific departments or roles).

## 4. Final Recommendation & Governance Limitations

### Recommended Candidate: Ensemble (Optimal w=0.55 LR + 0.45 Tuned RF)
- **Primary Metric Justification:**
  - **Highest PR-AUC:** 0.6576 CV / 0.6547 OOF (highest across all tested models).
  - **Highest Recall:** Detects **62.63% of attritions** at $p^*=0.28$ (119/190), compared to 56.32% for LR alone and 55.79% for RF alone.
  - **Highest Balanced F1:** **0.5965**, with solid precision (0.5694).
  - **Low Brier Score:** 0.0936.

### Secondary Candidate for What-If Policy Simulation: HistGB (Domain Full Monotonic)
- If the system is deployed specifically for interactive "what-if" policy simulation (e.g., simulating the effect of reducing overtime or increasing satisfaction), **HistGB (Domain Full Monotonic)** is recommended because it guarantees 0% directional violations across all employees.

### Known Limitations
1. **Uncalibrated Ensemble Outputs:** While the ensemble achieves superior rank-ordering (PR-AUC 0.6547), its probabilities are uncalibrated raw averages. Before production deployment, Platt Sigmoid calibration should be applied to align scores with enterprise base rates.
2. **Computational Overhead:** The ensemble requires executing two inference pipelines simultaneously (ColumnTransformer + LogisticRegression and ColumnTransformer + RandomForest). Inference latency is $\approx 15$ms per sample (acceptable for batch/dashboard use).
3. **Observational Benchmark:** The underlying IBM HR dataset is synthetic and cross-sectional; features reflect associations, not causal retention guarantees.
